Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I cancel a booking?

Go to the "My Bookings" section and press the Cancel button next to your trip.

Is the hotel included in the price?

Yes. The price includes stay, local travel and breakfast. GST is added at checkout.

Can I book for a group?

Yes, you can add up to 8 travellers in a single booking.

What is the Premium package?

Premium adds better hotels, private transfers and one extra experience for 20% more.
